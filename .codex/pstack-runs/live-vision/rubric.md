# Design rubric

Score 1-5 each: (1) continuous frames without per-frame subprocess or AX reads; (2) exact device/action isolation and lifecycle safety; (3) bounded four-phone cost and slow-reader backpressure; (4) one shared producer, small public API, maintainable local Swift/Go integration; (5) deterministic live proof and clear freshness/errors. Native-source feasibility is a blocking gate. Same-family model panel lacks family diversity; use distinct models as available.
